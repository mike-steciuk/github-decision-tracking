# DEC-NNNN: Short decision title

## Problem statement

What problem or constraint requires a decision? Include relevant context and
success criteria.

## Proposal

What change is proposed? Be concrete enough for an implementer or coding agent
to follow.

## Decision

What did the team decide, and why?

## Short-term impact

What changes immediately? Include migration work, delivery risk, cost, and
affected teams or systems.

## Long-term impact

What does this commit the team to? Include maintenance, scalability,
operational, security, and reversibility considerations.

## Alternatives considered

What credible alternatives were considered, and why were they not selected?

## Replaces

List decision IDs this record supersedes, or `None`.

## Related decisions

List relevant decisions from this or a linked org, department, team, project,
or service decision set. Use qualified references such as `org:DEC-0042` so
the source can be resolved through `decisions/README.md`. Explain the
relationship when it is not obvious.

## Scoped overrides

List any broader decisions this decision intentionally overrides within its
declared scope, or `None`. For each override, state the qualified decision
reference, the exact scope of the exception, and why the broader direction is
not suitable here. An override does not supersede or invalidate the broader
decision outside this record's scope.

## Consequences and follow-up

List follow-up work, review dates, success measures, and known tradeoffs.
